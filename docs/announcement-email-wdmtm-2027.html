Save the date

NWPTF meets April 1, 2027, at the 22nd WDMTM Conference, Purdue University, West Lafayette, IN

Colleagues,

Our next in-person National Wild Pig Task Force meeting is confirmed for Thursday, April 1, 2027, at 8:00 a.m. We’ll meet at Purdue University in West Lafayette, Indiana, during the 22nd Wildlife Damage Management Training and Meeting (WDMTM), which runs March 29 – April 1.

The conference is also a great chance to share your work. If your research or management touches wild pigs, we’d love to see it on the program.

Call for papers and posters

WDMTM is accepting abstracts for oral and poster presentations on wildlife damage management and related topics.

  • Deadline: December 11, 2026, by 5:00 p.m.
  • Length: 250 words or fewer, as a Word or PDF file
  • Send to: Brian MacGowan at macgowan@purdue.edu
  • Decisions: authors will hear back by January 21, 2027

Priority goes to results from completed studies. Full guidelines are on the conference website.
