Create your NWPTF member profile

Colleagues,

The National Wild Pig Task Force has launched a member portal on our new website, and we’re asking everyone on this list to create a profile. It takes about two minutes and there is no password to remember.

Why we’re doing this: the Task Force has grown into an international network, and a single email blast no longer serves it well. Your profile lets you tell us what you work on (research, management, policy, outreach) and what you want to hear about, so you get the updates that matter to you and none that don’t. It also lets you join Task Force subcommittees and keep your own contact information current.

Create my profile

How it works:

  1. Click the button above (or go to nwptf.org and click Member Login, then “Become a member”)
  2. Fill in the short form: your role, areas of interest, subcommittees if you’d like to join any, and email preferences
  3. Enter your email and click “Create my profile” — no password needed
  4. Open the email that arrives from members@nwptf.org and either click its sign-in button or type the code from it into the website. If it isn’t in your inbox within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder (and marking it “not spam” helps everyone after you)

A note for those of us on agency email systems: if the button in the sign-in email doesn’t work, that’s your agency’s security software. Use the code instead — that’s exactly what it’s for.

You control everything: update your info anytime, change what emails you receive, or remove yourself entirely, all from your account page. How we handle your information is spelled out plainly in our privacy notice.

Please take the two minutes this week. The more complete this network is, the more useful it becomes for all of us.

Nathan Snow
Chair, National Wild Pig Task Force
