Colleagues,

Some exciting news: the Task Force has a new website, and with it a member portal built for this community. Your member profile is already started — we brought the member list over. All we’re asking is that you sign in and finish it: about two minutes, and there is no password to remember.

This group has grown from a regional working group into an international network of researchers, managers, and policy folks, and we want our communication to grow with it. Your profile is the foundation: tell us what you work on and what you want to hear about, and we can start sharing the right things with the right people, whether that’s new research in your specialty, subcommittee work, or news about upcoming meetings.

And a promise up front: we will always be respectful of your inbox. You choose exactly what you receive, you can change your mind anytime, and every email we send will have a reason to exist.

Complete my profile

How it works:

  1. Click the button above (or go to nwptf.org and click Member Login at the top of any page)
  2. Enter the email address this message arrived at and click “Email me a sign-in code”
  3. We’ll email you a code from members@nwptf.org. Type it into the box on the website and you’re in. If the email isn’t in your inbox within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder (and marking it “not spam” helps everyone after you)
  4. Review your profile and fill in the new parts: your role, areas of interest, subcommittees if you’d like to join any, and email preferences. Hit Save and you’re done

Coming back later is the same two steps every time: Member Login, enter your email, type the code we send. No password, nothing to remember.

If the site says it can’t find your email, you may be reading this at a different address than the one on our list — just use “Become a member” on the sign-in page instead and build your profile fresh.

You control everything from your account page: update your info, change what you receive, or remove yourself entirely. How we handle your information is spelled out plainly in our privacy notice.

Please take the two minutes this week. The more complete this network is, the more useful it becomes for all of us. More to come soon.

Nathan Snow
