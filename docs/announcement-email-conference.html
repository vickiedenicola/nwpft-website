Stay connected to the wild pig community

Colleagues,

You joined us at the Wild Pig Conference, and we’d like to keep you in the loop. The Task Force has a new website and a member portal built for this community, and we’re inviting you to create a free member profile. It takes about two minutes, and there is no password to remember.

The Task Force has grown from a regional working group into an international network of researchers, managers, and policy folks. A profile is how you plug in: tell us what you work on and what you want to hear about, and we’ll share the right things with you, whether that’s new research in your specialty, news about future conferences and events, or what the Task Force and its subcommittees are working on.

And a promise up front: we will always be respectful of your inbox. You choose exactly what you receive, you can change your mind anytime, and every email we send will have a reason to exist.

Become a member

How it works:

  1. Click the button above (or go to nwptf.org, click Member Login at the top of any page, then “Become a member”)
  2. Fill in the short form: your role, areas of interest, subcommittees if you’d like to join any, and email preferences
  3. Enter your email and click “Create my profile”
  4. We’ll email you a sign-in code from members@nwptf.org. Type it into the box on the website and you’re done. If the email isn’t in your inbox within a couple of minutes, check your spam or junk folder (and marking it “not spam” helps everyone after you)

Coming back later is the same two steps every time: Member Login, enter your email, type the code we send. No password, nothing to remember.

You control everything from your account page: update your info, change what you receive, or remove yourself entirely. How we handle your information is spelled out plainly in our privacy notice.

Membership is free and open to anyone working on wild pigs, anywhere in the world. We’d be glad to have you in the network. More to come soon.

Nathan Snow
Chair, National Wild Pig Task Force
