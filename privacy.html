Privacy

How we handle your information

Effective August 19, 2026

What we collect

When you create a member profile, we ask for your name, professional title, company or affiliation, country, email address, optional phone number, your areas of interest, the NWPTF committees you'd like to join, other working-group affiliations, and your email preferences. That's the whole list — we don't collect anything else, and every field except your name and email is optional.

Why we collect it

One reason: so the Task Force can connect wild pig researchers, managers, and policy folks around the world, and email you the kinds of updates you asked for — and nothing else. We may also use aggregate counts (for example, "how many members work on disease surveillance") that never identify anyone.

What we never do

We do not sell member information. We do not share the member list outside the Task Force's own communications. Your profile is not published anywhere on this site.

Where it lives and who can see it

Profiles are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States, protected by row-level security: you can see and edit only your own profile, and only Task Force administrators can view the member list. Passwords are stored only in securely hashed form — nobody, including us, can read them.

Your choices and rights

  • Update anything anytime from your account page.
  • Change what we send you — or choose "No emails" — in your email preferences.
  • Delete your account or request a copy of your data by contacting us; we'll act on it promptly.

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, these are your access, rectification, erasure, and portability rights under the GDPR, and the same contact route applies.

Cookies and analytics

The site uses Google Analytics to understand overall visitor traffic (pages viewed, general region) and Supabase stores a session token in your browser so you stay signed in. We don't use advertising cookies.

Service providers

Three vendors process data to run this site: Supabase (member database and sign-in), Cloudflare (website hosting), and Google Analytics (site traffic). Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

Questions

Anything unclear, or a request we haven't covered? Get in touch and a real person will respond.
