Wild pigs don’t recognize boundaries

Wild pigs damage crops, degrade water quality, threaten native wildlife, and carry diseases that affect livestock and people. Their range continues to expand, and no single agency or state can manage the problem alone.

The Task Force exists to coordinate that response, connecting the research, the practitioners, and the policy on one continent-wide front.
