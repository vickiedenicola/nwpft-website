## Attribution notes
- **CC BY 2.0** (`news-screwworm.jpg`) requires a visible credit. Suggested line:
  "Photo: USDA, CC BY 2.0." A site-wide credits/colophon link is sufficient.
- **Public domain** USDA images carry no legal attribution requirement; crediting
  the agency is good practice.
- **NWPTF gallery** images are the organization's own assets, used in its own site
  redesign.
- The logo (`assets/nwptf-logo*.png`) is the NWPTF organizational mark, recovered
  at full resolution from nwptf.org.
