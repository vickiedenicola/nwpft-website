## Attribution notes
- **iStock** images are used under a standard iStock license; no public attribution is required.
- **CC BY 2.0** (`news-screwworm.jpg`) requires a visible credit: "Photo: USDA, CC BY 2.0." The credits page satisfies this.
- **Public domain** USDA images carry no legal attribution requirement; crediting the agency is good practice.
- **NWPTF gallery / org assets** are the organization's own images, used in its own site redesign.
